Rules. Daily Heikin Ashi candles, EMA 200 and EMA 300 on the HA close. Long: the HA candle broke below both lines, then the whole HA body (open and close) is above both; enter at that day's real close. Short: the mirror, from above both to below both. A one-candle cross of both lines counts. Part of the HA body past the second line counts as a break of it; a dip that never breaks the second line is not a signal. No signal when the EMAs are flat and tangled (within 1% of each other and the EMA 200 moved under 2% in 20 days). Stops and returns use real prices. Stop = beyond the far line × (2% + EMA gap): lower EMA × (1 − buffer) for longs, upper EMA × (1 + buffer) for shorts. Fixed at entry, hit on the daily low (longs) or high (shorts). No exit rule: each column is the return if held that long, or the stop loss if hit first. Short returns are shown as the short's profit.
